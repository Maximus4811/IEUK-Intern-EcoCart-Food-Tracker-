Chicken & Egg Fried Rice
Asian · 20 min · Beginner
Step-by-step Instructions
1
Dice the chicken breast into 2 cm pieces and season lightly with salt and pepper.
2
Heat 1 tbsp cooking oil in a non-stick pan or wok over high heat.
3
Fry the chicken for 5–6 minutes until golden and cooked through. Set aside.
4
In the same pan, crack in the eggs and scramble until just set. Push to one side.
5
Add cooked rice and stir-fry on high heat for 2 minutes, breaking up any clumps.
6
Return the chicken, add 1 tbsp soy sauce, mix everything together for 1 minute.
7
Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve immediately.
